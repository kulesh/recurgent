> Jaffna Kool is a traditional refreshing drink from the Jaffna region of Sri Lanka. Here's a classic recipe:

**Ingredients:**

*For the base:*
- 1 cup palmyra root (kool root/nungu), diced
- 1/2 cup sago pearls or tapioca pearls
- 1/4 cup agar agar jelly, cubed (or grass jelly)
- Water for boiling

*For the syrup:*
- 1 cup jaggery (palm sugar) or brown sugar
- 1 cup water
- 2-3 cardamom pods, crushed
- A pinch of nutmeg powder (optional)

*For serving:*
- Coconut milk (fresh or canned)
- Ice cubes
- Rose water (optional, few drops)

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the sago:** Boil sago pearls in water until translucent (about 10-15 minutes). Drain and rinse with cold water. Set aside.

2. **Prepare the jelly:** If using agar agar powder, prepare according to package instructions, let it set, then cut into small cubes.

3. **Make the syrup:** In a saucepan, combine jaggery and water. Heat until jaggery dissolves completely. Add cardamom and nutmeg. Let it cool and strain.

4. **Prepare palmyra root:** If using fresh palmyra root, clean and dice. If using canned, drain and rinse.

5. **Assemble:** In serving glasses, add a spoonful of sago, palmyra root cubes, and jelly cubes.

6. **Add syrup:** Pour the jaggery syrup over the ingredients (adjust sweetness to taste).

7. **Add coconut milk:** Pour chilled coconut milk over the top.

8. **Finish:** Add ice cubes and a drop or two of rose water if desired.

9. **Serve:** Mix gently before drinking and enjoy this cooling, sweet beverage!

**Tips:**
- Adjust sweetness according to your preference
- Some variations include adding fresh fruit like mango or banana
- The drink should be served very cold
- You can find palmyra root (nungu) at Asian grocery stores, often canned

This drink is especially popular during hot weather and is a beloved street food item in Jaffna!
